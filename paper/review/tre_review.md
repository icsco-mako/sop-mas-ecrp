This manuscript investigates end-to-end analytics based on natural language processing. First, the method is tested on ComplexOR and NLP4LP datasets. Then it has been implemented to an empty container repositioning problem.

I think this manuscript has very limited contributions to the domain and solution methods. I recommend desk rejection. My detailed comments:

1) The first 24 pages have no content about transport and logistics. These first 24 pages just explain a well known LLM MAS integration. There is no contributions to the method knowledge.
2) Empty container repositioning problem is a combinatorial optimisation problem. There are hundereds of papers delivering optimization algoirhtms (exact) to solve this problem. Your study cannot compare these exisiting models with your suggested framework.
3) Your insights and results about the problem are very limited (only one page). There is no sensitivity analysis to uderstand the impact of input variability on outputs.
4) CPLEX and Gurobi solve similar problems more efficiently, see exact solution methodsJetbrains Mono,  for container flow optimisation in 10.1016/j.omega.2024.103220.
5) Did you obtain APPENDIX C from published work? This is unclear.
