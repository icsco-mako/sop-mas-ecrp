\noindent Dear Editor,

\vspace{0.8em}

Thank you for the opportunity to submit a second revision of our manuscript \textbf{ASOC-D-25-13526R2}, entitled ``Optimization of Empty Container Repositioning by Integrating Large Language Models and Multi-Agent Systems.'' We sincerely thank all three reviewers for their insightful comments and valuable suggestions, which have substantially strengthened our manuscript. The major revisions include:

\begin{itemize}\itemsep2pt
  \item \textbf{Notation and presentation (Reviewer~3):} Unified the agent naming convention (full names with symbolic notation, e.g., ``Project Manager Agent ($a_{\text{PM}}$)''); defined variable $x_{ij}$; and formalized the Structural-Agreement Rate (SAR) with all tuple components defined and a worked example.

  \item \textbf{Narrative and framework transparency (Reviewer~4):} Reorganized the Literature Review around an ECR-first narrative detailing single-agent failure modes; added Section~3.2 (Inter-Agent Communication Protocol) and a structural justification of the sub-sequence rule; and formalized the state parameters and agent operational boundaries.

  \item \textbf{Formal complexity and efficiency analysis (Reviewer~4):} Added Section~3.4 deriving $T_{\text{orch}}=O(K\cdot|\mathcal{A}|)$, and expanded Section~4.4 with a token-overhead study (Table~3, Figure~5) reporting LLM calls and token consumption per paradigm.

  \item \textbf{Experimental interpretation and conclusion (Reviewer~4):} Clarified the solver-execution variation across equivalent encodings (Section~5.4); decomposed the 12.18\% cost reduction to isolate the lateral-land-route effect (Section~5.3); repositioned human-in-the-loop as a core architectural layer (Section~6); and incorporated four reviewer-suggested references.
\end{itemize}

A detailed point-by-point response is provided in the accompanying Response Letter. We believe the revised manuscript is now suitable for publication in \textit{Applied Soft Computing}.
